Modelling Activities

Over the years I have used and contributed to developments in different community models including Meso-NH, GEOS-Chem and mostly the ICTP RegCM5 regional climate model for processes relevant to climate, biogeochemistry and atmospheric chemistry.

Contribution to GEOS-Chem

The Modal Aerosol Model (Liu et al., 2012), interacting with the Model for Simulating Aerosol Interactions and Chemistry (Zaveri et al., 2008), is coupled with the GEOS-Chem chemical transport model. This enables a size-resolved representation of aerosol processes at a lower numerical cost relative to sectional approaches, while also offering compatibility for coupling with GCMs such as the Community Earth System Model. In our workflow, MAM4-MOSAIC is a library derived from the code version reported by Wu et al. (2024) E3SM implementation. It has been coupled with GEOS-Chem's chemistry, emissions, transport, deposition, and radiative processes (including RRTMG and Cloud-J). A box model version, operated through a Python Dash interface, has also been developed for ideal cases.
